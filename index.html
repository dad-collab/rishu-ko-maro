How to Play — Phase 6

Phase 6 adds a computer opponent (Player vs Computer) on top of Phase 5: signature special moves (one per fighter, cooldown-gated) and best-of-3 rounds on top of Phase 4 combat: punches, kicks, blocks, dodges and combos, with live health. These are the controls that actually work right now.

Movement (works now)

  • Player 1: WASD move · Space jump · Shift run.
  • Player 2 (Two Player Local): ↑←↓→ move · Right Shift jump · Enter run.
  • Touch: left joystick to move (push to the edge to run) · JUMP button on the right. The joystick drives Player 1 only.
  • Pause: P or Esc, or the Pause button in the match bar.
  • Your fighter always faces the opponent; the camera holds a fixed side view and keeps both fighters in frame.

Arena camera (works now)

  • Fixed side view — the classic fighting-game angle, steady through the whole fight. No follow, no zoom — just a subtle tremor on heavy hits.
  • Outside a match, the camera slowly orbits on its own (cinematic idle).
  • On phones, the fight plays in landscape — rotate your device so both fighters stay big.

Menus (work now)

  • Play Now → Game Modes → Character Select → The Arena.
  • Character cards — tap to select; stats are shown on every card.
  • Settings — sound and graphics quality, saved on this device.
  • Fullscreen — toggles real fullscreen on supported browsers.

Character select (works now)

  • Tap a fighter card — the live 3D preview switches with a spin animation.
  • Drag the preview — rotate the fighter; Auto-rotate and Walk Preview buttons control the animation.
  • Select Fighter — confirms the pick (victory pose + ✓ badge). Continue enters the arena with that fighter.
  • Your last confirmed fighter is remembered on this device and pre-focused next time.

Combat (works now)

  • Player 1: J light punch · K heavy punch · U kick · I heavy kick · L block (hold; L+S low guard) · O dodge · S+O backward evade · H special.
  • Player 2 (Two Player Local): Numpad 1 light punch · 2 heavy punch · 3 kick · 0 heavy kick · Numpad Enter block · Numpad 5 dodge · Numpad 6 special (alt N). Fallback: , . / ' attacks, ; block, M dodge.
  • Touch: LP / HP / KICK / HK buttons, hold BLOCK, tap DODGE, tap SP for the special — Player 1 only, beside the joystick.
  • Damage is fixed per fighter (strength and defense change it) — no random numbers. Blocking reduces a hit to small chip damage; dodging through an attack avoids it completely. Chain hits quickly to build a combo — the combo counter appears at 2+ hits and damage scales down after the second hit.
  • At 0 HP a fighter loses the round — first to 2 rounds wins the match, and the WINNER screen offers Rematch or Quit. A special is cooldown-gated (6–12 s by move); a blocked special deals small chip damage and a whiffed special still spends its cooldown.

Computer opponent (works now)

  • Player vs Computer — pick your fighter, then pick your CPU opponent: tap another fighter and press Select Fighter, or leave it random (Random CPU opponent button). Set the difficulty below the slots: Easy (slow reactions, basic attacks, rarely blocks), Medium (balanced timing, some blocks and dodges, basic combos) or Hard (fast reactions, counters, advanced combos). Difficulty changes how the CPU fights, not how hard it hits — damage is the same on every level.
  • The CPU watches the fight: it closes distance, attacks in range, blocks and dodges, punishes whiffs, and switches between aggressive, defensive, balanced and counter fighting as the match changes. It never reads your keys — it reacts to what your fighter does.
  • Beat the CPU and the screen says YOU WIN; lose and it says YOU LOSE. Rematch keeps the same difficulty; Change difficulty goes back to character select.

About RISHU.COM

RISHU.COM is an original premium 3D browser fighting game. All branding, characters, UI and visuals are original creations — nothing is copied from any other game.

Phase 1: main menu, game modes, character selection, How to Play, Settings, fullscreen, loading screen, and the 3D arena preview (Three.js / WebGL).

Phase 2: six original fighters with distinct procedural 3D models, a live rotatable character preview, idle / walk / victory / selection animations, and a confirmed-pick flow that carries into the arena.

Phase 3: movement (walk, run, jump), a fixed side-view fight camera, touch controls, a match timer and pause.

Phase 4: combat — punches, kicks, blocks, dodges, combos, deterministic damage, live health bars and KO.

Phase 5: signature special moves (one per fighter, cooldown-gated — Ember Rush, Storm Barrage, Iron Counter, Shadow Step, Forge Slam, Cyclone Kick) and best-of-3 rounds: first to 2 rounds takes the match, with a WINNER screen.

Visual Upgrade: tournament stadium with a live crowd, banners and jumbotron; rebuilt fighter models (proportions, faces, elbows, knees, hands); theatre-mode full-screen fights with a FIGHT banner; impact effects and a victory celebration. Combat is unchanged.
