How to Play — Phase 3

Phase 3 adds movement: walk, run, jump, a fight camera and touch controls. These are the controls that actually work right now.

Movement (works now)

  • Player 1: WASD move · Space jump · Shift run.
  • Player 2 (Two Player Local): ↑←↓→ move · Right Shift jump · Enter run.
  • Touch: left joystick to move (push to the edge to run) · JUMP button on the right. The joystick drives Player 1 only.
  • Pause: P or Esc, or the Pause button in the match bar.
  • Your fighter always faces the opponent; the camera follows the fight and keeps both fighters in frame.

Arena camera (works now)

  • Drag with mouse or finger — adjust the viewing angle around the fight.
  • Scroll / pinch — zoom in and out.
  • Outside a match, the camera slowly orbits on its own (cinematic idle).

Menus (work now)

  • Play Now → Game Modes → Character Select → The Arena.
  • Character cards — tap to select; stats are shown on every card.
  • Settings — sound and graphics quality, saved on this device.
  • Fullscreen — toggles real fullscreen on supported browsers.

Character select (works now)

  • Tap a fighter card — the live 3D preview switches with a spin animation.
  • Drag the preview — rotate the fighter; Auto-rotate and Walk Preview buttons control the animation.
  • Select Fighter — confirms the pick (victory pose + ✓ badge). Continue enters the arena with that fighter.
  • Your last confirmed fighter is remembered on this device and pre-focused next time.

Not in Phase 3

  • Combat controls (punch / kick / block / special) — a later phase.
  • Health bars, rounds, win/lose — a later phase. The health bars in the match bar are labelled placeholders for Phase 5.
  • CPU opponent AI — a later phase (in Player vs Computer the CPU fighter stands in guard for now).
  • Online Multiplayer — coming in a later phase.

About RISHU.COM

RISHU.COM is an original premium 3D browser fighting game. All branding, characters, UI and visuals are original creations — nothing is copied from any other game.

Phase 1: main menu, game modes, character selection, How to Play, Settings, fullscreen, loading screen, and the 3D arena preview (Three.js / WebGL).

Phase 2: six original fighters with distinct procedural 3D models, a live rotatable character preview, idle / walk / victory / selection animations, and a confirmed-pick flow that carries into the arena.

Phase 3: movement (walk, run, jump), a third-person fight camera, touch controls, a match timer and pause. Fighting — punches, blocks, health, rounds — is a later phase.

What’s next?

  • Real combat: punch, kick, block, dodge, special moves.
  • Health bars, round timer, best-of rounds, win / lose screens.
  • CPU opponent AI for Player vs Computer.

Movement, camera, touch controls, the match timer and pause are already in — Phase 3, live now. Online Multiplayer stays Coming Soon until the netcode phase.
