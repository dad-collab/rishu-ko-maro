How to Play — Phase 1

Phase 1 is the foundation: menu, characters and the 3D arena preview. These are the controls that actually work right now.

Arena camera (works now)

  • Drag with mouse or finger — orbit the camera around the ring.
  • Scroll / pinch — zoom in and out.
  • Leave it alone — the camera slowly orbits on its own (cinematic idle).

Menus (work now)

  • Play Now → Game Modes → Character Select → Arena Preview.
  • Character cards — tap to select; stats are shown on every card.
  • Settings — sound and graphics quality, saved on this device.
  • Fullscreen — toggles real fullscreen on supported browsers.

Not in Phase 1

  • Combat controls (punch / kick / block / special) — Phase 2.
  • Health bars, rounds, timer, win/lose — Phase 2.
  • Online Multiplayer — coming in a later phase.

Movement keys (WASD / arrows) are not active in Phase 1 — the fighters are preview stands, not controllable yet.

About RISHU.COM

RISHU.COM is an original premium 3D browser fighting game. All branding, characters, UI and visuals are original creations — nothing is copied from any other game.

Phase 1: main menu, game modes, character selection, How to Play, Settings, fullscreen, loading screen, and the 3D arena preview (Three.js / WebGL).

Phase 2 (planned): real fighting — punches, blocks, health, rounds, timer, win/lose, and keyboard + touch controls.

What’s in Phase 2?

  • Real combat: punch, kick, block, dodge, special moves.
  • Health bars, round timer, best-of rounds, win / lose screens.
  • Keyboard controls (P1) and touch controls on mobile.
  • CPU opponent AI for Player vs Computer.
  • Two Player Local with separate key sets.

Online Multiplayer stays Coming Soon until the netcode phase.
